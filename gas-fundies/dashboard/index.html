Monthly balance (Bcf/d), weekly storage, Henry Hub price, rig counts. Free data only: EIA API, EIA weekly storage files, Baker Hughes. LNG feedgas is an estimate: LNG exports × 1.11 (liquefaction fuel and losses). The balancing item is shown, never plugged.

Monthly balance — supply minus demand (Bcf/d)

Supply = dry production + pipeline imports + LNG imports. Demand = reported consumption + pipeline exports + LNG exports. Supply − demand should equal the change in storage; the gap to reported storage change is the balancing item.

Demand breakout (Bcf/d)

Consumption by sector, plus LNG feedgas estimated from LNG exports.

Dry production by region, estimated (Bcf/d)

EIA publishes dry production only for the US total; by state it publishes marketed production. Regional dry = regional marketed × the month's US dry/marketed ratio. Regions are state aggregates (Appalachia = PA/OH/WV; Southwest = TX/NM; Haynesville = LA; states are not basin-pure — see rig counts for basin detail).
